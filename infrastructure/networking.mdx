Every app on Antideploy gets a public URL the moment it's first deployed, secured with HTTPS by default. When you're ready to go live on your own domain, Antideploy handles SSL certificate provisioning and renewal automatically — you only need to add a DNS record.

## Default app URL

On first deployment, your app is assigned a URL in the format:

```
https://<app-name>.antideploy.app
```

This URL is permanent, always available, and secured with a wildcard SSL certificate. You can share it immediately without any configuration.

## Adding a custom domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your app's networking settings">
    In the Antideploy dashboard, open your app and click **Infrastructure → Networking**. Then click **Add Domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your domain">
    Type the domain you want to use (for example, `www.mycompany.com` or `app.mycompany.com`). Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your DNS records">
    Antideploy displays the DNS records you need to add at your domain registrar or DNS provider. You'll typically add one of the following:

    **CNAME record** (recommended for subdomains):

    | Type | Host | Value |
    |---|---|---|
    | CNAME | `www` | `cname.antideploy.app` |

    **A records** (required for apex/root domains):

    | Type | Host | Value |
    |---|---|---|
    | A | `@` | `198.51.100.1` |
    | A | `@` | `198.51.100.2` |

    Add these records at your DNS provider, then return to the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for DNS propagation">
    DNS changes typically propagate within a few minutes but can take up to 48 hours depending on your provider's TTL settings. Antideploy polls your domain automatically and notifies you when it detects the correct records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="SSL certificate is provisioned automatically">
    Once DNS is verified, Antideploy requests and installs a TLS certificate for your domain. The status indicator next to your domain turns green and your app is immediately accessible at the custom URL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You don't need to do anything to provision or renew SSL certificates. Antideploy handles the full certificate lifecycle. Certificates are renewed automatically before they expire.
</Note>

## SSL certificates

Antideploy provisions certificates through a trusted certificate authority automatically. All certificates are:

- **Issued automatically** when a domain is verified
- **Renewed automatically** before expiration — typically 30 days before the expiry date
- **Secured with TLS 1.2 minimum** — older TLS versions are rejected
- **HSTS-enabled** — browsers are instructed to always use HTTPS

You can view your certificate details (expiry date, issuer, domains covered) under **Infrastructure → Networking → Certificates**.

## HTTPS enforced by default

All HTTP traffic to your app is automatically redirected to HTTPS with a `301` permanent redirect. This applies to both your default `.antideploy.app` URL and any custom domains you add.

<Warning>
HTTP-to-HTTPS redirection cannot be disabled on Antideploy. All apps must serve traffic over HTTPS. If a client makes an HTTP request, it will always receive a redirect response.
</Warning>

## Adding multiple domains

You can add as many domains as your plan allows. All domains are served by the same deployment — there is no need to deploy your app multiple times. To add another domain, click **Add Domain** again from the **Infrastructure → Networking** page and repeat the DNS configuration steps.

<Tip>
If you want two domains to serve identical content, add both and let Antideploy handle routing. If you want one domain to redirect to another (for example, `mycompany.com` → `www.mycompany.com`), configure that as a redirect instead.
</Tip>

## Redirects and path routing

### Domain-level redirects

To redirect one domain to another permanently, go to **Infrastructure → Networking → Redirects** and click **Add Redirect**. Set the source domain and the destination URL. Antideploy returns a `301` response for all requests to the source domain.

For example, to redirect the apex domain to the `www` subdomain:

| From | To | Type |
|---|---|---|
| `mycompany.com` | `https://www.mycompany.com` | 301 (permanent) |

### Path-level routing

If your app exposes multiple services or microservices, you can route different URL paths to different Antideploy apps. Configure path rules under **Infrastructure → Networking → Routing**:

| Path prefix | Routes to |
|---|---|
| `/` | `main-app` |
| `/api/` | `api-service` |
| `/admin/` | `admin-app` |

Path routing rules are evaluated in order — more specific prefixes take priority. Changes to routing rules take effect within seconds and do not require a redeployment.

## Checking domain status

The **Infrastructure → Networking** page shows the current status of every domain attached to your app:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟡 **Pending DNS** | Antideploy is waiting to detect your DNS records |
| 🔵 **Issuing Certificate** | DNS verified; SSL certificate is being provisioned |
| 🟢 **Active** | Domain is live and HTTPS is fully configured |
| 🔴 **Error** | A problem was detected — click for details and next steps |

If a domain shows an error, click its row to open the diagnostic view. Common issues (incorrect DNS records, propagation delays, certificate issuance failures) are described with specific remediation steps.
