---
title: "Signing in"
description: "Sign in with GitHub, or with a one-time link sent to your email. There are no passwords."
---

Antideploy has no passwords. You sign in with an account you already have, or
with a link we email you.

## GitHub

**Continue with GitHub** is the quickest way in, and the one you need to
deploy from a repository: the same account connects your repositories.

## Email link

Enter your email address and click **Email me a sign-in link**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    The email comes from Antideploy and has one button. If it's not there within
    a minute, check your spam folder.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the link">
    It opens a page with a **Continue** button. Opening the link alone doesn't
    sign you in, so an email scanner that visits links can't use it up before
    you do.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Continue">
    You're signed in. If the address doesn't have an account yet, one is created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A link works once and expires after 15 minutes. You can ask for up to 5 links an
hour for the same address.

<Note>
**Signed up with Google?** Use the same email address as your Google account,
and the link signs you in to that same account, with all your apps.
</Note>

If your account was created with GitHub only, an email link won't sign you in
to it. We send a note telling you to use **Continue with GitHub** instead,
because GitHub accounts can carry an email address their owner never verified.

## Google

**Continue with Google** is temporarily unavailable. Use an email link with your
Google address instead: it reaches the same account.

## Signing in an AI agent

Agents and scripts don't use this page. See [terminal login](/api/terminal-login)
for how an agent gets an account token.
