App Intelligence is an AI layer built into Antideploy that develops a deep understanding of your application over time. It continuously ingests your codebase, logs, performance metrics, error traces, cost data, and deployment history to build a live model of how your app behaves — and what it needs to run better.

## What App Intelligence monitors

App Intelligence watches every dimension of your running application:

- **Errors and exceptions** — runtime errors, unhandled rejections, crash loops
- **Performance metrics** — request latency, throughput, database query times, memory and CPU usage
- **Deployment history** — which deploys introduced regressions, rollback patterns, failed builds
- **Infrastructure costs** — per-resource spend, over-provisioned capacity, idle compute
- **Log patterns** — anomaly detection across structured and unstructured logs
- **Dependency health** — outdated packages, known CVEs, upstream API failures

## Accessing App Intelligence

Open the **Intelligence** tab in your app's control center dashboard. From there you can:

- View the live health summary for your app
- Trigger or schedule diagnostic runs
- Browse and apply recommendations
- Review the AI's action history and audit trail

<Tip>
  App Intelligence starts building context the moment you deploy. The longer your app runs, the more accurate its analysis becomes — most apps see meaningful recommendations within 24 hours of first deployment.
</Tip>

## Permission levels

You control how much authority App Intelligence has to act on your behalf. Set the permission level from **Intelligence → Settings** in the dashboard.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Read-Only" icon="eye">
    The AI monitors your app and surfaces findings in the dashboard but takes no action and makes no suggestions. Useful for auditing without interference.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Recommend" icon="comment">
    The AI analyzes your app and presents recommendations and diagnostic reports. All actions require your explicit approval before anything changes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Auto-Fix with Approval" icon="circle-check">
    The AI proposes specific fixes — config changes, resource adjustments, code patches — and applies them only after you approve each one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fully Autonomous" icon="robot">
    The AI detects issues and applies approved categories of fixes automatically, without requiring per-action approval. Every action is logged in the audit trail.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Fully Autonomous mode applies only to the fix categories you explicitly enable — for example, resource scaling or dependency updates. It will never modify application code without your configuration permitting it.
</Note>
