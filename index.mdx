Antideploy is the deployment and operations platform for apps built anywhere — with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, traditional development, or any low-code tool. Bring your existing application through GitHub, a folder upload, the CLI, or an API call, and Antideploy handles everything else: analyzing your codebase, provisioning infrastructure, building and deploying your app, and keeping it running in production.

## Get Up and Running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Sign up at [app.antideploy.io](https://app.antideploy.io). No credit card required to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your app">
    Link a GitHub repository, upload a ZIP, or run `antideploy deploy` in your project directory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Antideploy analyzes and builds">
    The platform detects your framework, language, and dependencies, then provisions the exact infrastructure your app needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your app is live">
    Get a production URL with HTTPS, a control center dashboard, logs, metrics, and everything else — ready to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Already inside Claude Code, Cursor, or another AI coding environment? Install the [MCP integration](/integrations/mcp) to deploy, check logs, and diagnose issues without leaving your editor.
</Tip>
